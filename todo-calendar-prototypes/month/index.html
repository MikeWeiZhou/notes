Duration Grid: 10 variations

Ten takes on the Duration Grid month view, each handling busy days and phone screens differently. They show a copy of your app's tasks and projects (or sample tasks if the app has none in this browser), so ticking things off here won't change the app. Try each one on your phone too.

  1. Open each one and play with it
  2. Tap ★ Pick to tick what you like
  3. Choose one as the month view
  4. Copy your picks below and send them
