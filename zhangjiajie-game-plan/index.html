Short answer

Give your hours to Yuanjiajie 袁家界

  • Most time: Yuanjiajie 袁家界. The Avatar pillar 乾坤柱, the natural bridge 天下第一桥 and Mihun Terrace 迷魂台 sit on one paved cliff path of about 2 km, and the Bailong Elevator 百龙天梯 down is at its far end. Travellers short on time say to put it first. Posts
  • Light touch: Tianzi Mountain 天子山. Line A makes you change buses at Helong Park 贺龙公园 anyway. Give it 20 to 30 minutes for the Imperial Brush Peaks deck 御笔峰, then go. Posts
  • Skip: Huangshizhai 黄石寨. It stands apart by the south gate. Its cable car links to no bus or trail on the plateau you will be on, and travellers who saw Yuanjiajie first found it less dramatic. Posts
  • The train 十里画廊小火车 fits two ways. Last, after the elevator down, which is the order the park itself suggests (route 2). Or at midday, before riding the elevator up to the pillar (route 3). Plan to board by 16:30. Last rides
  • My pick: route 1. Decide about the train when you step out of the elevator, using the clock below.
Before you go in

The place in five minutes

  • What you are looking at. More than 3,000 quartz sandstone pillars, many over 200 m tall. Over 1,000 of them pass 120 m and 45 pass 300 m. Guinness lists it as the largest concentration of sandstone pillars on Earth.
  • How it formed. About 380 million years ago this was a shallow Devonian sea laying down sand. The sand hardened into thick quartz sandstone and was lifted into a plateau. Water then worked down the vertical cracks, helped by frost and tree roots, and cut the plateau into walls, then fins, then free-standing pillars. Tianzi Mountain 天子山 and Yuanjiajie 袁家界 are what is left of the plateau top. The pillars are what is left of its edges.
  • Why the mist matters. Forest covers about 98% of the park and the air is humid. After rain, cloud pools in the gorges and the pillar tops stand clear above it. Friday was wet, so today has a chance of that.
  • The names. Wulingyuan 武陵源 is the whole protected area. Zhangjiajie National Forest Park 张家界国家森林公园, founded in 1982, was China's first national forest park. Jie 界 means a boundary or domain, so Zhangjiajie is the Zhang family's land, and Yuanjiajie and Yangjiajie follow the same pattern. Local legend says the Zhang was Zhang Liang 张良, a Han dynasty strategist who retired here.
  • The people. This is Tujia 土家族 country. The stories you will hear on Tianzi Mountain, of a rebel king and his stone brushes, are Tujia legends.
  • Recognition. UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1992, listed for natural beauty, and a Global Geopark since 2004. The highest ground is on Tianzi Mountain, at about 1,262 m.
  • The film. Avatar opened in December 2009. A month later the park renamed one pillar after the film's floating mountains. The film was shot on soundstages, and its designers also drew on Huangshan 黄山, so the claim rests on reference photos.
  • The residents. Wild macaques work the valley floor, most of all at Ten-Mile Gallery 十里画廊. They take food from hands and open bags.

    Why: a natural rock arch joining two pillars, and the start of the cliff path. View it from the deck first, then cross it.

    Hallelujah Mountain 乾坤柱 · 哈利路亚山

    Why: the pillar behind the floating mountains in Avatar. This is the stop the day is built around.

    Watch out: the densest crowd of the day. Wait for a gap at the rail instead of shooting over heads.

    Guide: The Chinese name is Qiankun Pillar 乾坤柱, the pillar of heaven and earth. English guides used to call it the Southern Sky Column. Critics in China objected to the foreign name and officials had to defend it. Nothing was filmed here: the floating mountains were built on computers from reference photos. When mist fills the gorge the base vanishes and the column seems to hang in the air, which is the look the film borrowed.

    Back Garden 后花园 Optional walk

    The quiet corner posters keep naming, a request stop on the bus to the elevator. Only if you reach Mihun Terrace by 14:15.

    Watch out: one guide lists the path as closed for repairs. Ask the driver before getting off.

    Guide: The path drops through bamboo until a rock wall seems to block it. At the corner a round, moon-shaped opening leads through, and dozens of pillars appear in a dark green ravine.   8. 15:357
    Bailong Elevator, down 百龙天梯 下行

    Buy the down ticket (¥65, passport). 326 m in about 90 seconds. Stand at the glass side or you see nothing.

    Watch out: November waits ran from none to an hour. Be in the queue by 15:45.

    Guide: Bailong means Hundred Dragons. Work began in 1999 and the lift opened in 2002. The cars travel 326 m. The lower half runs in a shaft drilled into the cliff, and the upper 170 m or so climbs an exposed steel tower. Guinness certified it as the world's tallest outdoor lift in 2015. It was controversial from the start, bolted onto a World Heritage cliff, and it shut for ten months in 2002–03 over safety. The park's case is that it let them clear the hotels off the plateau.

    Mini-train bonus 十里画廊小火车 Optional

    The bus to the gate passes Ten-Mile Gallery. If you reach that stop by 16:00, hop off and ride the train in and back (¥76). Board by 16:30.

    Guide: Ten li is about 5 km, the length of this valley. It is called a gallery because the cliff line reads like a scroll of rock figures. Watch for the Longevity Star Welcoming Guests 寿星迎宾 near the start and the Old Man Gathering Herbs 采药老人, a stooped figure with a basket on his back. At the end stand the Three Sisters 三姐妹峰. In the local telling the eldest carries a baby on her back, the second holds one in her arms and the youngest is expecting. A tram has run here since 1999, and the zodiac-themed cars arrived in 2022.

The lower half runs in a shaft drilled into the cliff, and the upper 170 m or so climbs an exposed steel tower. Guinness certified it as the world's tallest outdoor lift in 2015. It was controversial from the start, bolted onto a World Heritage cliff, and it shut for ten months in 2002–03 over safety. The park's case is that it let them clear the hotels off the plateau.

    What posts say
    ThenBusto the Mihun Terrace stop 迷魂台站 · 5–10 min · from the elevator top
  6. 14:456
    Mihun Terrace 迷魂台

    Why: the all-round platform, and one poster's tip is to come after 15:30 for softer light and fewer people. Twenty minutes.

    Guide: Mihun means losing your soul. The old line is that visitors were so dazed by the view they forgot where they were. More than a hundred pillars rise out of the gorge below, and locals have named many of them. Look for the Heavenly Dog Watching the Moon 天狗望月 and the Generals in Formation 将军列队.

    What posts say
    ThenWalkto Hallelujah Mountain 乾坤柱 · 10–15 min · paved cliff path, some steps
  7. 15:107
    Hallelujah Mountain 乾坤柱 · 哈利路亚山

    The Avatar pillar. Twenty minutes, then walk back the way you came to the Mihun Terrace bus stop by 15:45.

    Guide: The Chinese name is Qiankun Pillar 乾坤柱, the pillar of heaven and earth. English guides used to call it the Southern Sky Column. About 150 m of it stands free, with the top around 1,080 m above sea level. On 25 January 2010, five weeks after Avatar opened, the park renamed it Avatar Hallelujah Mountain 阿凡达-哈利路亚山. Critics in China objected to the foreign name and officials had to defend it. Nothing was filmed here: the floating mountains were built on computers from reference photos. When mist fills the gorge the base vanishes and the column seems to hang in the air, which is the look the film borrowed.

    What posts say

  8. +20＋
    First Bridge Under Heaven 天下第一桥 Optional walk

    Carry on to the bridge and take the 10-minute road back to the Mihun bus stop. Only if you leave Hallelujah Mountain by 15:20.

    Guide: The usual explanation is that a slab of harder sandstone stayed put while water cut away the rock beneath it. What is left is an arch about 20 m long and 2 to 3 m wide. Sources put the drop under it anywhere from 357 to 400 m. It is one of two natural bridges in Wulingyuan.

What posts say about each stop

Why travellers recommend each place and what they warn about, with links. Sources are Tripadvisor reviews, Trip.com Moments, Ctrip, Douban, Weibo and personal blogs. Summaries are paraphrased.

Yuanjiajie, the mountaintop to prioritise 袁家界

Why go
The famous pillar views line one paved, railed path, so tired legs get the most scenery per step. Posters who had to choose put it first.
Watch out
Narrow decks jam when tour groups arrive, and you reach it in the afternoon when some views are backlit.
  • Personal blog · Pete R, BucketListly · Jan 2026: if time is short, drop Tianzi and Yangjiajie and give it all to Yuanjiajie.
  • Personal blog · Pete R, BucketListly · Jan 2026: ranks Yuanjiajie first, Tianzi second, Yangjiajie third.
  • Personal blog · David and Intan, The World Travel Guy · Aug 2025: with a late start, do only Yuanjiajie and Tianzi. The Yuanjiajie trail takes one to two hours and is signed in English.
  • Ctrip travel note · fswpq · visited 7 Nov 2024: also entered the East Gate at 11:00 and went up the Tianzi cable car. Spent about two hours on Yuanjiajie in the late afternoon.
  • Personal blog · Fabio Nodari · Nov 2026: the dissent. Short on time, he would head straight to Tianzi.

Huangshizhai 黄石寨 Skipped

Why skip
Posters like it, but as a quiet second-day stop. It sits by the south gate, its loop takes two to three hours, and nothing connects it to Yuanjiajie or Tianzi on top.
The other view
Several travellers call it peaceful and worth the trip if you have an extra day.
  • Personal blog · Ken Lawrence, Sailingstone Travel · May 2019: the least remarkable of his three days. A flat loop of about three hours, for visitors with a spare day.
  • Personal blog · Panos, Unusual Nomad · Apr 2026: worth seeing, as a second-day item.
  • Personal blog · Mimi · updated Nov 2025: a good add-on, but not linked to the other upper paths.
  • Personal blog · Michaela, Backpacking People · 2018: worth it, and far quieter than Yuanjiajie.
  • Guide site · China for Travelers · Sep 2026: calls it the park's most skippable zone. Its cable car rises from the south gate and joins nothing on the plateau.
  • Guide site · Wander in China · 2026: visitors who went after Yuanjiajie and Tianzi found it a little less dramatic.

East Gate and Line A 标志门 · A线

Why it works
Line A means cable car up, elevator down. Posters credit it with lighter queues than the reverse.
Watch out
The lane and slot must match the booking. There is a staircase before the cable car.
  • Trip.com Moments · OlgaMay · 2026: picked Line A as far less crowded. A free eco-bus runs from the turnstile to the Tianzi cable car.
  • Tripadvisor · Shopfoot P · Nov 2026: booked the wrong line and missed the slot. A clerk moved them to the next one.
  • Personal blog · Hey Roseanne · 2026: ten minutes late for the slot, fixed by staff in two minutes.
  • Personal blog · Serena's Lenses · Sep 2026: prefers Line B for seeing the pillars first, and notes Line A's staircase up to the cable car.

Tianzi Mountain cable car 天子山索道

Why go
The low-queue way up outside holidays, with views over the pillars from the cabin.
Watch out
Tickets are sold per direction. Afternoon queues build for the ride down, but route 3 rides down at midday.
  • Trip.com Moments · Lee Lee Sam · Dec 2025: about 10 minutes and ¥72 one way. Crowds grow on the way down in the afternoon.
  • Tripadvisor · Blanches85 · Nov 2024: no cable car queue in November.
  • Personal blog · Pete R, BucketListly · Jan 2026: had a cabin to himself on a late-autumn visit.
  • Personal blog · Maura Lydon · Mar 2020: the ride is worth it. The summit area is small.

Helong Park: Imperial Brush Peaks 贺龙公园 · 御笔峰

Why go
The Imperial Brush deck is the one Tianzi view posters call unmissable, and it sits beside the bus change you make anyway.
Watch out
You arrive after the morning light posters favour. Photo queues run 13:00 to 16:00. One poster found Fairy Scattering Flowers underwhelming.
  • Personal blog · Pete R, BucketListly · Jan 2026: Imperial Brush and Fairy Scattering Flowers are enough. There is little else up here.
  • Ctrip travel note · fswpq · Nov 2024: liked Imperial Brush Peaks, was let down by the Fairy deck, and gave up on Dianjiang Terrace after a 30-minute shuttle wait.
  • Tripadvisor · lmeuskadi · Apr 2026: calls the Imperial Brush deck a must-see.
  • Personal blog · Lauren Neves · May 2025: take the stairs right of the statue for framed views. McDonald's, Luckin and stalls on the square.
  • Sina blog · 落霞孤鹜 · Apr 2019: walked the decks as one short loop. Photo queues from 13:00 to 16:00.

First Bridge Under Heaven 天下第一桥

Why go
A natural stone arch between two pillars. The bus from Tianzi stops here, so it opens the cliff walk.
Watch out
No bus links this stop with Mihun Terrace. The stretch between is on foot.
  • Ctrip travel note · 清风乐语 · May 2025: walked bridge, Hallelujah pillar, then Mihun Terrace, the same order as these routes.
  • Personal blog · ZED67 · Apr 2025: no bus between the bridge stop and Mihun Terrace.
  • Trip.com Moments · Nov 2025: see the whole arch from the deck first, then cross it and look back.
  • Tripadvisor · LG_Kay · Oct 2016: ride to the First Bridge stop and tell the driver.

Hallelujah Mountain 乾坤柱

Why go
The film pillar. Every Yuanjiajie account includes it.
Watch out
Crowds. Decks fill from mid-morning and weekends are worst. Today is a Saturday.
  • Personal blog · Ummi Goes Where · Jul 2026: on a November visit the decks got busier by mid-morning, weekends most of all.
  • Tripadvisor · LG_Kay · Oct 2016: expect a crush along the cliff walk.
  • Ctrip travel note · 清风乐语 · May 2025: the full walk took over three hours with detours.

Mihun Terrace 迷魂台

Why go
All-round view over the pillar forest. Several posters name it their favourite platform.
Watch out
Selfie crowds at the rail, and mixed reports on afternoon light.
  • Trip.com Moments · several posters: 360-degree views and one poster's favourite platform. Another says go after 15:30 for light and fewer people.
  • Trip.com Moments · ExpeditionEnthusiast#7 · Jul 2025: best light in the morning, backlit in the afternoon.
  • UDN blog · loveabba · Jan 2025: photographed it from 15:30 to 16:30 in slanting sun.
  • Personal blog · Sailingstone Travel · 2019: had to wait out the selfie crowd.

Back Garden 后花园 Optional

Why go
The quiet Yuanjiajie corner. Posters describe half the crowds a few minutes off the road.
Watch out
May be shut. One guide lists it as closed after a path collapse. No post times the detour reliably.
  • Trip.com Moments · AMV. GGGG: half the crowds, a five-minute detour from the parking area.
  • Personal blog · Groovy Mashed Potatoes · 2019: their favourite spot, with barely anyone there.
  • Guide site · TravelChinaGuide: lists Back Garden as inaccessible while a collapsed footpath is repaired.

Bailong Elevator 百龙天梯

Why go
The fast way down: 326 m in about 90 seconds, straight to the valley buses.
Watch out
Only the glass side sees out. Queue reports for the descent run from empty to an hour.
  • Ctrip travel note · fswpq · visited 7 Nov 2024: went up at about 15:10 with few people queuing and rode down at about 17:40 with almost nobody in line. A crowd formed at the bottom near 18:00, likely for the bus.
  • Personal blog · Mimi · updated Nov 2025: the queue down is much shorter than the queue up.
  • Ctrip reviews · 2026: the moment the car leaves the shaft is the highlight. Stand by the window, and expect an hour or more at peak times.
  • Tripadvisor · reviews from Nov 2025 to Nov 2026: ¥65 down, passport needed. Waits ran from almost none late in the day to an hour.
  • Tripadvisor · Blanches85, Nov 2024 · Alessandro, Sep 2025: from the back you see nothing, and the windows are dirty.

Only one dated November report covers the ride down, and none is from a Saturday.

Ten-Mile Gallery and mini-train 十里画廊 · 小火车

Why go
A flat valley under the pillars you just stood on, with a seated ride. Liked by tired walkers.
Watch out
Opinion is split: several posters say walk it and skip the train, and one found the valley repetitive. Macaques snatch food at the far end.
  • Trip.com Moments · TheSofaView: sit left going in, since the right side is mostly rock wall. 10 to 15 minutes one way.
  • Weibo article · Nov 2026: after 16:00 the train has almost no queue, so it can go last in the day.
  • Trip.com Moments · Annabb3 · Feb 2025: train in for the commentary, walk back in about 20 minutes. ¥38 one way.
  • Personal blog · Serena's Lenses · Oct 2026: a relaxed alternative to walking, with the same views.
  • Personal blog · Ummi Nadrah · Aug 2026, mid-November visit: about 30 minutes each way on foot, flat.
  • Ctrip travel note · fswpq · Nov 2024: walked halfway and turned back, finding it repetitive.
  • Personal blog · Pete R, BucketListly · Jan 2026: the walk is very easy, so skip the train.
  • Ctrip reviews · 2025 to 2026: only the edge seat sees anything. Monkeys wait by the far stop, so hide food.

Dianjiang Terrace 点将台 Not in the routes

Why go
Almost nobody stops there. Two posters rate the view above Imperial Brush Peaks.
Watch out
Request stop. Shuttle waits of 20 to 30 minutes are reported.
  • Trip.com Moments · Isaac Diaz: few visitors, and a better view than Imperial Brush Peak.
  • Personal blog · Michaela, Backpacking People · 2018: nearly empty, with the best views of her visit.
  • Douban · ENJOY · Sep 2023: say the stop name to the driver when boarding.
  • Ctrip travel note · 遥远的景象 · Jun 2024: waited over 20 minutes at the next stop as full buses passed.

Know before you go

  • Slot and line must match your booking. The turnstile checks both. Travellers who arrived late or on the wrong line say staff re-slotted them in minutes.
  • Last rides are set on the day. Plan on 17:00 for the lifts and 16:30 for boarding the train. The table above has the detail. Ask at the cable car base: 今天最后一班几点？
  • Valley buses run in segments. From the elevator bottom to Ten-Mile Gallery and the East Gate you change at Shuiraosimen 水绕四门.
  • Request stops exist on top. Tell the driver your stop or the bus drives past. Full buses skip stops, so re-boarding mid-route can take 20 minutes.
  • Dress for the top. Forecasts put Tianzi Mountain and the elevator area at about 10 to 12°C, windier than town. Steps may still be wet from Friday.
  • Fog check at Helong Park. Humidity was near 100% this morning and no source reports summit visibility. If the top is whited out when you arrive, take route 3: it puts the train first and gives the cloud three hours to lift.
