Sat Feb 6: arrive HNL 21:40

AC1785 lands at 21:40 at HNL Terminal 2. Allow about 45 min to reach the rental counters at the consolidated rent-a-car facility across from Terminal 2, then about 20 min to drive to Waikiki. Counter hours found: Enterprise and National until 23:00, Hertz until midnight, Avis until 01:00. Book a brand open to midnight or later, add the flight number to the booking, and send the two drivers ahead while the others wait for bags.

Fri Feb 12: leave for HNL

AC1784 departs 22:55 from HNL Terminal 2; Air Canada checks in at Lobby 4. Bag drop closes 21:55; counters open about 19:55. Return the rental cars at the consolidated rent-a-car facility across from Terminal 2 by about 19:45 (allow 30 min to hand back the cars and walk over). Driving from the hotel area in Waikiki: leave by 19:00 (about 20 to 30 min on H-1 plus 10 to 15 min to refuel near the airport); 20:00 is the latest sensible start. Times are estimates; check live traffic on the day, because Friday rush hour can run past 18:00.

Fri Feb 12: bags after checkout

Recommended: check out in the morning and leave every bag with the Aston Waikiki Sunset front desk, which lists baggage storage and is staffed 24 hours; when checking in on 6 Feb, confirm it will hold about 10 to 14 bags until 19:00 on the 12th and ask about any charge. Keep the cars empty all day, collect the bags at about 18:30 after the early dinner nearby, and drive straight to the airport. Fallback: Bounce Luggage Storage (Honolulu Zoo) at 2584 Lemon Rd, about 300 m from the hotel and listed as open 08:00 to 22:30, or Waikiki Baggage and Luggage Storage at 119 Ohua Ave, also about 300 m away and listed 07:00 to 20:00. The airport's Baggage Storage by Smarte Carte near Baggage Claim 18 (from $12 per carry-on and $15 per large case per 24 hours, as found Nov 2026) is listed as closing at 20:00, so it only works if the bags are collected before then. Never leave bags in a parked rental car, even in the trunk; break-ins at beaches and lookouts are a known problem on Oahu.
