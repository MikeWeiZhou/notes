Six ways to spend an 11am start

You enter at the East Gate on Line A, which sends you up the Tianzi Mountain cable car. That leaves about six hours of daylight. Five of the six routes include the Ten-Mile Gallery mini-train. All clock times are estimates and shift with queues. On 14 Nov the plan was checked against traveller posts: each route carries a verdict, and each stop links to who recommends it.

Checked 14 Nov · traveller posts

What the posts change

  • Train-first routes (4 and 6) are unconfirmed. Line A visitors describe being bussed from the turnstile straight to the cable car. Nobody reports reaching Ten-Mile Gallery first. Ask at the bus bay; if the answer is no, fall back to route 1 or 5.
  • Yuanjiajie takes longer. Travellers spent 2 to 2.5 hours on the cliff walk, and the bus over from Tianzi Mountain runs 45 to 75 minutes with waits.
  • Route 3 used the wrong trail. First-hand accounts walk down to Three Sisters Peak in 1.5 to 2.5 hours. The route now follows them.
  • Treat 17:00 as the last ride. No post or notice confirms anything later for mid-November. Elevator queues in November reviews ran from none to an hour.
  • Route 5 is the safest. Cable car both ways is confirmed on Line A. You need separate up and down tickets.

The train you asked about

Ten-Mile Gallery mini-train 十里画廊小火车

  • ¥38 one way, ¥76 return. Not part of the park ticket. Pay by WeChat, Alipay or cash. Foreign cards are generally not accepted at the booth.
  • 10 to 15 minutes each way along a 2.5 km valley to Three Sisters Peak 三姐妹峰.
  • Sit on the left going in, in the outer seat. That side is open and faces the named rocks. Middle seats see little, and the ride back looks through a window.
  • A flat boardwalk runs beside the track. Walking one way takes 20 to 40 minutes. The way in climbs gently, so posts advise train in, walk out. Several say skip the train and walk both ways.
  • Aim to board by 15:45. Posts list 07:00 or 07:30 to 18:00, and one guide gives 17:00 for November to February. No post confirms when queues peak.

Who recommends each viewpoint

Traveller posts found on 14 Nov 2026, with why they rate each stop. Reddit, Xiaohongshu, Douyin and YouTube could not be opened, so these are Tripadvisor reviews, Trip.com Moments, Ctrip, Douban and personal blogs. Summaries are paraphrased.

East Gate and Line A 标志门 · A线

Posts confirm Line A means cable car up, elevator down, and credit it with lighter crowds. Most independent travellers still prefer the reverse order.

  • Trip.com Moments · OlgaMay · 2026: picks Line A as far less crowded. After the Line A turnstile a free eco-bus runs to the Tianzi cable car.
  • Tripadvisor · Shopfoot P · Nov 2026: booked the wrong line and missed the slot. A clerk moved them to the next one.
  • Personal blog · Hey Roseanne · 2026: ten minutes late for the slot, fixed by staff in two minutes.
  • Personal blog · Serena's Lenses · Sep 2026: prefers Line B for seeing the Avatar pillars first, and notes Line A has a staircase up to the cable car.

Tianzi Mountain cable car 天子山索道

Recommended as the low-queue way up outside holidays, with a strong view on the way down in evening light.

  • Trip.com Moments · Lee Lee Sam · Dec 2025: about 10 minutes and ¥72 one way. Afternoon crowds build on the way down.
  • Tripadvisor · Blanches85 · Nov 2024: no cable car queue in November.
  • Personal blog · BucketListly · Jan 2026: had a cabin to himself on a late-autumn visit.
  • Personal blog · Lauren Neves · May 2025: evening light on the ride down is excellent.
  • Douban · ENJOY · Sep 2023: rode the cable car up and down the same day.

A Nov 2025 reviewer on the same Tripadvisor listing waited over an hour at two points on the mountain.

Helong Park: Imperial Brush Peaks and Fairy Scattering Flowers 御笔峰 · 仙女散花

The Imperial Brush deck is the view posters call unmissable on Tianzi. The two decks sit on one short loop, with food on the square.

  • Tripadvisor · lmeuskadi · Apr 2026: calls the Imperial Brush deck a must-see.
  • Personal blog · Lauren Neves · May 2025: take the stairs right of the statue for framed views. McDonald's, Luckin and stalls on the square.
  • Personal blog · BucketListly · Jan 2026: Imperial Brush Peak is best in morning light, and the Fairy deck faces east.
  • Sina blog · 落霞孤鹜 · Apr 2019: walked Fairy, Imperial Brush and the pavilion as one loop. Photo queues from 13:00 to 16:00.

You arrive around noon, after the morning light these posts favour and at the start of the photo queues.

Tianzi Pavilion 天子阁

Rated for the all-round view from its upper tiers, not for the building. Needs clear air.

  • Personal blog · Mariana Dias · Jul 2025: highest deck on Tianzi and an easy walk from Helong Park.
  • Trip.com Moments · chloemarvel: six-storey platform, recommended at sunset.
  • Trip.com Moments · ReveLunaireSerein · Oct 2025: looks over Imperial Brush Peak and is at its best in mist.
  • Personal blog · Sailingstone Travel · 2019: 360-degree views from the tiers.

Dianjiang Terrace 点将台

Recommended because almost nobody stops there. One poster rates the view above Imperial Brush Peaks.

  • Trip.com Moments · Isaac Diaz: few visitors, and a better view than Imperial Brush Peak.
  • Trip.com Moments · chloemarvel: quieter than the main decks. Ask the driver to stop.
  • Douban · ENJOY · Sep 2023: say the stop name to the driver when boarding.
  • Tripadvisor · Robin S · May 2016: buses skip minor stops unless told, and packed buses make re-boarding hard.
  • Ctrip travel note · 遥远的景象 · Jun 2024: waited over 20 minutes at the next stop as full buses passed.

Walk-down trail to Three Sisters Peak 卧龙岭

Recommended for solitude: posters met almost no one. They also call it steep, tiring and short on views.

  • Tripadvisor · David C · Sep 2024: about 600 m of descent in 1.5 to 2 hours on good paths, very few people.
  • Tripadvisor · Blanches85, Nov 2024 · Jenn S, Jul 2021: 90 minutes, tiring, few views. Two hours of steep steps with no vendors.
  • Personal blog · BucketListly · Jan 2026: about 2.5 hours from Helong Park to the gallery, fewer than ten people on the trail.
  • Personal blog · And They Go: the South Heaven Gate branch is a very long staircase, hard on legs.
  • Personal blog · Sailingstone Travel · 2019: the South Heaven Gate branch came out near the lower cable car station.
  • Tripadvisor · Konstantin I · 2017: steps are slippery when wet.

No post confirms the trail is open today. Ask at Helong Park before starting.

First Bridge Under Heaven 天下第一桥

Posts use it as the start of the cliff walk. The bus from Tianzi stops here, and the stretch on to Mihun Terrace has to be walked.

  • Ctrip travel note · 清风乐语 · May 2025: walked bridge, Hallelujah pillar, then Mihun Terrace, the same order as this plan.
  • Personal blog · ZED67 · Apr 2025: no bus links the bridge stop and Mihun Terrace.
  • Autohome forum: off at the bridge stop, walk the cliff path, bus from the Mihun stop to the elevator.
  • Tripadvisor · LG_Kay · Oct 2016: ride to the First Bridge stop and tell the driver.

Hallelujah Mountain 乾坤柱

Nobody argues for it; every Yuanjiajie account includes it as the film pillar. The advice is all about crowds.

  • Tripadvisor · LG_Kay · Oct 2016: expect a crush along the cliff walk.
  • Personal blog · Ummi Goes Where · Jul 2026: on a November visit, decks got busier by mid-morning, weekends most of all.
  • Ctrip travel note · 清风乐语 · May 2025: the full walk took over three hours with detours.

No post supports the earlier tip about quieter side platforms.

Mihun Terrace 迷魂台

Named as a favourite platform for its all-round view. Posts split on whether afternoon light helps or hurts.

  • Trip.com Moments · several posters: 360-degree views and one poster's favourite platform. Another says go after 15:30 for light and fewer people.
  • Trip.com Moments · ExpeditionEnthusiast#7 · Jul 2025: best light in the morning, backlit in the afternoon.
  • UDN blog · loveabba · Jan 2025: photographed it from 15:30 to 16:30 in slanting sun.
  • Personal blog · Sailingstone Travel · 2019: had to wait out the selfie crowd.

Back Garden 后花园 Not in the routes

The quiet Yuanjiajie spot posters keep naming. Add it only if you are ahead of schedule: one poster puts the plank road to Mihun Terrace at about 40 minutes.

  • Trip.com Moments · AMV. GGGG: half the crowds, a five-minute detour from the parking area.
  • Personal blog · Groovy Mashed Potatoes · 2019: their favourite spot, with barely anyone there.

Bailong Elevator 百龙天梯

Rated as the fast way down more than as a view. Queue reports for the descent vary widely.

  • Tripadvisor · reviews from Nov 2025 to Nov 2026: ¥65 down in about 90 seconds, passport needed. Waits ran from almost none late in the day to an hour, and three hours in one October report.
  • Tripadvisor · Blanches85, Nov 2024 · Alessandro, Sep 2025: from the back you see nothing, and the windows are dirty.
  • Trip.com Moments · traveller1993, travelwithteetee: hours given as 08:30 to 18:00 off-season. Queues can reach two hours later in the day.
  • Company blog · Eventurus Asia: about ten minutes to descend on a June weekend in Line A order.

Ten-Mile Gallery and mini-train 十里画廊 · 三姐妹峰

Liked as a flat, easy valley after the summits, and for tired legs, kids and older visitors. Opinion on the train is split: several say walk and skip it.

  • Trip.com Moments · TheSofaView: sit left going in, since the right side is mostly rock wall. 10 to 15 minutes one way over about 2 km.
  • Personal blog · Serena's Lenses · Sep 2026: the scenery is all on the left. A free bus links the gallery with the cable car lower station.
  • Tripadvisor · lmeuskadi, May 2026 · SOH KIEN PENG, Sep 2025: an ideal flat walk after the summits. ¥76 return, not in the park ticket, about 30 minutes on foot end to end.
  • Trip.com Moments · Annabb3 · Feb 2025: train in for the commentary, walk back in about 20 minutes. ¥38 one way.
  • Trip.com Moments · Ekaterina___st: underrated, and best in late-afternoon light.
  • Personal blog · BucketListly · Jan 2026: the walk is very easy, so skip the train.
  • Ctrip reviews · 2025 to 2026: only the edge seat sees anything. Monkeys wait by the far stop, so hide food.
  • Trip.com Moments · Trip_V: the way in is a gentle climb, and monkeys past the small bridge snatch snacks.

Some posters were upset by caged monkeys near Three Sisters Peak. The path runs right beside the track, so it is not a quiet walk.

Know before you go

  • Slot and line must match your booking. The turnstile checks both. Two travellers who missed a slot or booked the wrong line say staff re-slotted them within minutes. One guide describes Line A as the left lane.
  • Check what your Trip.com order includes. Line A fixes your direction only. The cable car (¥72) and elevator (¥65) are separate tickets unless your voucher lists them.
  • Last rides are set on the day. The elevator usually stops at 18:00, or 17:00 in winter. The cable car runs to 17:00 in low season. No notice confirms mid-November times, so treat 17:00 as the limit and be in your last queue by 16:00. The park notice lists a hotline: 0744-5712345.
  • Request stops exist. On the mountaintop buses, tell the driver where you want to get off or the bus drives past. Full buses skip stops too, so re-boarding mid-route can take 20 minutes.
  • Passport for ride tickets. Recent reviewers needed it to buy the elevator ticket at the gate.
  • Dress for the top. The plateau is colder and windier than Wulingyuan town. Bring a layer and shoes with grip.
  • Monkeys live in the gallery. Macaques gather near Three Sisters Peak. Keep food zipped in a bag.
  • Mist cuts both ways. After Friday's rain you may get clouds drifting between the pillars, or a whiteout. If the top is socked in at noon, the train-first routes keep you below the cloud while it lifts.
