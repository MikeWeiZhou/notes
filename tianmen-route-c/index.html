Your guide says · the way up and down

Think of the way up as four acts. A cabin lifts you over the 99 bends; a short level walk brings the arch into view; the square gives you the picture; then seven escalators carry you up through the rock. The moment not to rush is the square: ten minutes, the whole arch, all the steps. The story is AD 263, when by tradition the cliff fell away and opened Heaven's Gate. Morning fog often hides the arch and tends to lift late morning, and you pass the cave again coming down. The way down is the same line in reverse: 75 minutes from joining the queue to the gate.

    Leave the hotel by Didi 滴滴 温德姆花园酒店

    Checked out, bags left at the desk. Order the car a few minutes earlier. Destination 山门, about 10 to 15 min.

    Guide note · 天门山 Tianmen Mountain

    Welcome to 天门山, Heaven's Gate Mountain. The main peak stands 1,518.6 m, about 8 km from the city centre: a limestone table ringed by cliffs, nothing like Wulingyuan's sandstone pillars. Its old name was Songliang 嵩梁山. Tradition: a cliff split open in AD 263 and the Wu ruler Sun Xiu, taking it for a good omen, renamed the mountain. Tourism material calls it the soul of Zhangjiajie; that is a slogan, not a measurement. Expect the top to be 5 to 10 °C colder than town.

    Guide note · 山门 Mountain Gate and the closed long cableway

    This gate is where Route C begins and ends: tickets are checked here and the express cableway leaves from here. Look for the long cableway.     Guide note · 快线索道 Express cableway

    The express line is reported to have entered regular service in March 2020: a three-rope (3S) gondola, 26 people to a cabin, reported at 1,209 m long and about 4 minutes, against roughly 25 for the bus.     Guide note · 登天门扶梯 Lower escalator, square to cave

    If the steps are not for you, this is the alternative: the lower escalator group beside the staircase, from the square up to the cave.     Guide note · 上天梯 Stairway to Heaven, 999 steps

    999 steps: about 300 m of staircase rising 150 m, laid out as five gentler stretches and four steep ones, up to about 45 degrees at the steep end. Tradition: nine stands for eternity, so 999 reads as 'everlasting'. Look for the love locks along the sides. In February 2018 a Range Rover was driven up all 999; in November 2025 another car attempting it slid back into the balustrade. Guides give 15 to 30 minutes for the climb. Mind your footing when the stone is wet.

    Guide note · 天门洞 Tianmen Cave, Heaven's Gate

    The standard figures for this arch are 131.5 m high, 57 m wide and 60 m deep; a 1999 survey gave 127.37 m. Altitude: about 1,260 m, or 'over 1,300' elsewhere. The record says the cliff collapsed in AD 263. On 11 December 1999 seven aerobatic pilots flew through, the Hungarian Peter Besenyei first. Legend: on the cliff to the right, water is said to burst out in dry weather once every ten-plus years. Cloud streaming through is 天门吐雾, Tianmen exhaling mist.

    Guide note · 穿山扶梯 Tunnel escalators, cave to summit

    Now we go inside the mountain. From behind the cave, seven escalator sections climb through a tunnel to the summit, and the return ride is in your ticket. The published figures describe the whole system from the square to the summit, twelve sections in all: 897 m of moving stairway lifting 340 m, opened on 25 April 2015, able to carry 3,600 people an hour each way. One newspaper called it the world's first escalator system inside a mountain tunnel. The approved plan had been a vertical lift shaft.

    Guide note · 天门山山顶 Summit plateau

    You are standing on a limestone table, not a sandstone pillar: an isolated block ringed by cliffs. The highest point is 1,518.6 m and the plateau averages above 1,400 m. It covers roughly 2 square km, about nine tenths of it forest. Expect it to be 5 to 10 °C colder than the city. The top is windy, so a light raincoat beats an umbrella, and wet boardwalks are slippery in smooth-soled shoes. On Route C you leave by the same seven escalators you arrived on.
